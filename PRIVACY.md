# Privacy policy: Exam Photo Resizer

Live page: https://photo.shaikhismail.com/privacy.html

## The short version

Exam Photo Resizer runs entirely in your web browser. Your photos and signatures are never uploaded to any server, never stored, and never shared with anyone.

There is no account, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts or fonts.

## What happens to your photos and signatures

When you choose a photo, your browser crops, resizes and compresses it on a canvas. The name and date you type for the strip are drawn onto the image in your browser and are not kept anywhere.

Everything stays in your browser's memory for that tab only. When you close or reload the tab, it is gone. The website itself is technically blocked from sending your data anywhere: its security policy does not allow the page to make any network requests after it loads.

## What is stored on your device

Only one thing, and only if you use it: when you switch between light and dark theme, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the page remembers it. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it any time from your browser settings.

## What the web server sees

Like every website, the hosting provider (MilesWeb) automatically keeps standard access logs when the page loads: your IP address, the date and time, the page requested and your browser type. These logs are used only to keep the site secure and running, are kept for a limited time, and never contain your photos and signatures or anything you type into the tool. We do not use them to identify or follow you.

## Children

The tool does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
