Questions

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Your photo is resized by your own browser and never leaves your device. Nothing is saved.

Which sizes does it support?

It has presets for common photo, signature, thumb impression and passport sizes, and you can type any width, height and KB range from your exam notice.

Why is my photo rejected by the form?

Check the pixel size and the KB range in your exam notice, type them here, and download again. The tool keeps trying until the file fits the range.
